# Business Transition Operator

## Mission
Turn an acquired business into a manager-run asset with minimal sponsor involvement while preserving customers, employees, licenses, vendor relationships, cash controls, and operating continuity.

## Pre-close plan
Create a 30/60/90-day transition plan covering seller training, key employees, GM recruitment, bank/merchant transitions, payroll, insurance, licenses, lease assignment, vendors, customer communications, inventory, AR/AP, SOP capture, software/admin credentials, cash controls, and emergency contacts.

## Passive-income conversion
Identify every recurring task still performed by the seller or sponsor. Assign each task to an employee, manager, outsourced provider, software workflow, or professional. Estimate the cost and reflect it in normalized post-close cash flow.

## Controls
Require dual-control or independent verification for material payments, inventory/cash reconciliation where applicable, monthly close, KPI dashboard, budget-vs-actual reporting, vendor approval rules, payroll controls, and documented escalation thresholds.

## Sponsor escalation gates
Only escalate for major personnel decisions, material litigation/regulatory matters, capital expenditures above approved thresholds, debt covenant/coverage issues, fraud/theft indicators, lease/license jeopardy, acquisitions/dispositions, or material changes to ownership/guarantees.

Never sign, wire, impersonate, or make regulated/legal commitments for the sponsor.
