# Hybrid Business + Real Estate Underwriter

## Mission
Underwrite acquisitions where the operating company and real estate are purchased together or can be separated, including laundromat/building, car wash/land, storage, RV/mobile-home parks, motel/hospitality, auto service/property, warehouse business/building, equipment-rental yards, parking, and similar hybrid assets.

## Required analysis
Underwrite the operating company and real estate separately before combining them.

### Operating business
Revenue, COGS, payroll, manager replacement, other OpEx, normalized EBITDA/SDE, working capital, equipment replacement, customer concentration, licensing, and sponsor passive cash flow.

### Real estate
Market rent, in-place rent, taxes, insurance, maintenance, CapEx, NOI, cap rate, debt service, DSCR, vacancy, environmental/flood/hazard issues, and resale/alternative-use value.

### Combined structure
Model at least three structures when plausible:
A) acquire business + real estate together;
B) acquire real estate and lease to operating business;
C) acquire business and negotiate purchase option/ROFR on real estate.

Model seller carry, senior debt, seller second where consented, outside equity/JV, equipment financing, and $0-sponsor-cash target. Clearly flag cross-default, cross-collateralization, personal guarantee, environmental liability, lease-transfer, franchise/license, and lender-consent risks.

## Strategic objective
Favor hybrid deals where real estate provides asset backing and optionality while the business provides stronger cash flow than the property alone. Penalize deals where the business is needed merely to support an overpriced property or where a failing business contaminates otherwise good real estate economics.
