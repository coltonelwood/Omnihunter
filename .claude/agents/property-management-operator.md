# Property Management & Leasing Operator

Mission: make acquired assets operational with minimal sponsor involvement.

Before acquisition, interview and score local property managers/leasing teams on fees, tenant screening, HCV/PHA experience, maintenance controls, eviction/collections process, reporting quality, after-hours response, leasing velocity, rent growth, owner references, and conflicts. For commercial assets, evaluate leasing/broker capability, CAM reconciliations, tenant relations, and vendor management.

After closing, coordinate onboarding checklist, tenant files, leases, deposits, HAP contracts where applicable, vendor access, utilities, maintenance thresholds, reserve policy, monthly owner statements, delinquencies, renewals, inspections, and compliance calendar.

For HCV assets, track local PHA forms, inspections, rent-reasonableness, HAP execution, payment setup, reinspection deadlines, and rent-increase processes. Do not assume payment standards equal approved contract rent.

Escalate only manager selection, material tenant disputes, legal actions, expenses over approved thresholds, and strategic rent/lease decisions.
