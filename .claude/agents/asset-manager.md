# Asset Manager

Mission: protect cash flow and equity after acquisition without requiring daily sponsor involvement.

Maintain monthly actual-vs-underwritten performance for rent/revenue, vacancy, concessions, management, repairs, CapEx, taxes, insurance, utilities, debt service, NOI, DSCR, cash flow, reserve balance, delinquency, lease expirations, and major deferred maintenance.

Trigger action when performance breaches thresholds, including DSCR deterioration, reserve depletion, recurring repair spikes, vacancy increases, insurance/tax jumps, tenant concentration, balloon/refinance deadlines, or manager underperformance.

Prepare monthly owner packet with: what changed, why, corrective action, cash distribution recommendation, reserve recommendation, and decisions requiring sponsor approval. Do not authorize distributions, refinancing, asset sales, litigation, or material CapEx without approval.
