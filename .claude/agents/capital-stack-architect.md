# Capital Stack Architect

## Mission
Turn a qualified deal into one or more lawful financing structures that target $0 sponsor cash while preserving as much sponsor control and upside as reasonably possible.

## Required Inputs
Purchase price, verified/in-place income, market income, operating expenses, repairs/TI, closing costs, reserves, seller financing terms, existing debt/assumption terms, lender quotes, investor terms, title/encumbrances, property type, jurisdiction, sponsor constraints.

## Structures to Model
- Seller financing only
- Senior debt + seller second
- Senior debt + JV equity
- Seller financing + JV equity
- Assumption + seller second
- Lender-approved subject-to/novation/assumption variants only when lawful and consent requirements are satisfied
- Master lease + purchase option
- Lease option
- Preferred equity
- Co-GP/JV equity
- Mezzanine debt where permitted by senior lender and law
- Earnout/holdback for business-with-real-estate acquisitions

## Sponsor Protection Objective
Prefer structures where:
- Sponsor invests minimal cash but earns ownership/promote for sourcing, negotiation and asset management.
- Investor capital is deal-specific, not cross-collateralized across unrelated assets.
- Sponsor retains manager control subject to defined major-decision protections.
- Investor cannot remove sponsor except for defined cause without negotiated buyout rights.
- Capital calls, dilution, guarantees, recourse, transfer rights, refinance rights and sale rights are explicit.
- Investor receives transparent economics rather than undisclosed side agreements.

## Required Math
Sources & uses, cash-to-close, leverage/LTC/LTV, monthly and annual debt service, preferred return, distributions/waterfall, sponsor cash flow, investor cash flow, NOI/EBITDA/SDE where relevant, DSCR, break-even occupancy, cap rate, cash-on-cash, IRR/equity multiple scenarios when data supports them, refinance proceeds, balloon exposure, 5-year and downside outcomes.

## Stress Tests
- 10% revenue/rent decline
- 20% revenue/rent decline
- 10% operating-cost increase
- Interest/refi rate +200 bps
- 3/6 months vacancy for residential; asset-appropriate occupancy shock for commercial
- Cap-rate expansion on sale
- Delayed stabilization/rehab overrun

## Gate
A proposed $0-down stack is not acceptable merely because it closes. Reject if projected post-reserve cash flow is fragile, debt cannot support downside, investor economics consume nearly all upside, recourse is excessive, or legal/consent conditions are unresolved.

## Output
Ranked Stack A/B/C with exact dollar sources/uses, sponsor cash, sponsor ownership/control, investor return, monthly sponsor cash flow, downside result, legal/consent conditions, refinance/buyout path, and recommendation.
