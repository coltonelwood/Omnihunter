# Manager-Run Business Underwriter

## Mission
Convert advertised business earnings into a realistic passive/semi-passive owner cash-flow model after fully replacing seller labor and funding normal working capital, maintenance, management, and debt service.

## Required normalization
Build seller case, normalized case, downside case, and transition-year case.

Explicitly separate:
- Revenue
- COGS
- Gross profit
- Direct payroll
- Manager/GM replacement compensation
- Payroll taxes/benefits
- Rent/CAM or mortgage occupancy cost
- Utilities
- Insurance
- Repairs/maintenance
- Equipment replacement reserve
- Marketing
- Software/admin
- Franchise/royalty fees
- Professional fees
- Working-capital needs
- Seller discretionary add-backs that are actually defensible
- EBITDA
- SDE
- normalized manager-run EBITDA
- debt service
- investor distributions
- sponsor cash flow

## Owner replacement rule
If the seller works materially in sales, service delivery, management, scheduling, bookkeeping, purchasing, customer retention, licensing, or technical production, price the cost of replacing every material role. If replacement would require multiple people, model multiple hires. Never call pre-replacement SDE passive income.

## Quality checks
Verify or demand tax returns, P&Ls, bank statements, merchant processor records, payroll filings, sales-tax filings, lease, equipment list, customer concentration, AR/AP aging, contracts, licenses, employee roster, normalized working capital, and capex history before treating earnings as verified.

## Metrics
Calculate purchase multiple on normalized EBITDA/SDE, DSCR or fixed-charge coverage, monthly passive sponsor cash flow, cash-on-cash after investor split, break-even revenue, downside margin, working-capital runway, equipment replacement exposure, and 5-year sponsor/investor economics.

## Kill criteria
Reject or re-trade if normalized manager-run cash flow is weak or negative, working capital is materially understated, lease/license transfer is uncertain, concentration is dangerous, seller add-backs are unsupported, capex is hidden, or debt/investor economics consume nearly all sponsor upside.
