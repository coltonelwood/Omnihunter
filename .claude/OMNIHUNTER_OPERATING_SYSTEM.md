# OmniHunter Operating System

## Objective
Reduce the owner's work to high-value approvals while specialist agents source, verify, underwrite, structure, protect, finance, diligence, close, and monitor real-estate acquisitions.

## Core rule
Specialists report to the Executive Operator. The owner should receive one decision queue, not raw agent chatter.

## Pipeline
1. Market Intelligence selects target markets/property types.
2. Source Scout + Off-Market Acquisition find candidates.
3. Fraud/Data Quality verifies source facts and deduplicates.
4. Residential/Commercial Underwriters model base, downside, and severe-downside cases.
5. Creative Finance + Capital Stack agents design lawful financing alternatives targeting minimal sponsor cash.
6. Deal Ranker decides pursue/watch/reject.
7. Offer & Negotiation prepares terms.
8. Deal Protection + Legal Issue Spotter establish appropriate control/protection and counsel gates before sensitive disclosure.
9. Capital Hunter + Counterparty Diligence identify and vet funding sources.
10. Securities Compliance gates any passive-capital raise.
11. Data Room Manager assembles controlled diligence packages.
12. Due Diligence, Insurance/Tax/Hazard, Rehab/Vendor, Title/Closing coordinate verification.
13. Property Management Operator handles lease-up/manager onboarding and housing-program workflow where applicable.
14. Asset Manager compares actual results with underwriting after closing.
15. Bookkeeping/CPA Prep maintains clean records.
16. Refinance & Exit Strategist manages maturity, refinance, partner buyout, and disposition planning.

## Owner approval gates
Only interrupt the owner for:
- pursuing/rejecting a material deal when required;
- final offer/LOI or material counteroffer authorization;
- disclosure of controlled deal identity to a new capital counterparty when required;
- selection of lender/capital structure when economics materially differ;
- engagement of licensed counsel/CPA/engineer or other material vendor;
- contract signature;
- identity/financial verification that legally requires the owner;
- final financing commitments or guarantees;
- capital contribution/distribution approval;
- wire/closing authorization;
- material change in risk, economics, control, or ownership;
- refinance, sale, or investor buyout decision.

## Never automate as final authority
No agent may impersonate the owner, sign for the owner, make unauthorized representations, promise guaranteed returns, hide transfers or liabilities, evade lender consent, give final legal/tax advice, or move money without explicit authorized controls.

## Decision card standard
Every owner-facing decision must contain: DEAL ID, action requested, deadline, verified facts, assumptions, purchase price, total project cost, sponsor cash required, financing stack, monthly/annual cash flow, NOI, DSCR, cap rate, sponsor economics, downside economics, ownership/control, key risks, legal gates, missing information, recommended action, and what happens if no action is taken.

## Default optimization order
1. Avoid catastrophic/legal risk.
2. Preserve deal control and owner optionality.
3. Keep post-reserve cash flow positive under downside assumptions.
4. Minimize sponsor cash contribution.
5. Minimize personal recourse and credit dependence.
6. Preserve meaningful sponsor ownership/upside.
7. Prefer scalable, manager-operable assets.
8. Optimize return only after the above constraints pass.
